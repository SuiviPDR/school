About My School

A Large & Wonderful School, Close to My Heart

Anassi School, located in the city of Meknes, Morocco, is a place I study in and hold with great pride. To me, it is not just a place I go to every day for lessons, tests, and exams — it is an educational space where I gain knowledge and skills, develop my abilities, and meet teachers who contribute to my academic growth.

It is a large and wonderful institution, distinguished by its vast space, its many classrooms, and a suitable environment for studying. What makes it truly special, however, is not only its size — but the level of education I witness there and the dedicated teachers who give their best to help students learn and progress.

  • ✓ A spacious, large campus with numerous classrooms
  • ✓ A complete secondary program from Grade 10 to the Baccalaureate
  • ✓ Dedicated teachers for a wide variety of subjects
  • ✓ A place where ambitions are built and knowledge is gained
  • ✓ A welcoming environment that encourages hard work and discipline

Our Location

Conveniently located in Anassi, Meknes — easy to recognize thanks to a well-known nearby landmark.

📍

Anassi, Meknes

Anassi School is situated in the heart of the Anassi district of Meknes, Morocco. The location is easy to recognize thanks to its proximity to a well-known reference point in the area.

Nearest Landmark: Right next to Jawharat Al-Ouloum Library (مكتبة جوهرة العلوم) — a famous and beloved library in the Anassi district, making the school easy to find and describe.
